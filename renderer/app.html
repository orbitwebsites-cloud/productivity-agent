WhatsApp Remote (beta)

Text Pesto on WhatsApp and it answers/acts using the exact same brain as this app — "what was I working on today?", "focus on Tech Job for 45 min", plus a couple of !git dev-ops commands.

⚠️ This uses an unofficial WhatsApp Web connection (not Meta's paid API), so it's free — but automating a personal WhatsApp account this way is against WhatsApp's Terms of Service and Meta can ban the linked number. Only messages sent from your own linked phone are ever acted on. Off by default; enable at your own risk.

Browser Control (beta)

Let Pesto drive your browser for you — "open the FAQ and tell me the refund policy", "find the cheapest flight to NYC next Friday". It reads the active tab, then clicks, types, and navigates a few steps to get it done, reporting back when it's finished.

Hard limit, always on: Pesto never clicks Submit, Buy, Pay, Subscribe, or Delete. It stops and hands those back to you. Everything runs on your machine — nothing about your browsing leaves this computer.

Connect your browser (one time)

  1. Click Open extension folder below.
  2. In Chrome/Edge: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick that folder. (Firefox: about:debugging → This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on.)
  3. Keep a browser window open — the dot above turns green when it connects.
