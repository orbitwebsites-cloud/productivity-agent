Privacy FAQ

What Pesto sees, and what never leaves your machine.

?

Does Pesto record my screen?

No. Pesto tracks only the active app name and window title — never pixels. A future opt-in “Full” tier for screenshots will be per-app and off by default, never automatic.

?

Where is my activity stored?

100% locally, in a plain file on your own computer. The free tier — tracking, Life Pursuits, Jarvis Mode, and deterministic answers — needs no account and nothing ever leaves your machine.

?

What does Premium send anywhere?

Only when you ask Pesto a question with AI answers unlocked: a small text summary (app names, window titles, durations) goes to our server to get phrased. Never screenshots, never your full activity log, never without you asking.

?

Is this okay on a work laptop?

We'd recommend personal machines only. Even metadata-only tracking can run against an employer's device policy — that's a policy question, not something a privacy toggle can fix for you.
