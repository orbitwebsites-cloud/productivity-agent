Built for the real failure mode

Most blockers assume you are logical right when you are not.

Pesto is designed around the moment you start drifting: tired, stressed, bored, or switching tabs without remembering why.

Not a punishment wall

Pesto warns first, then minimizes. It does not shame you, lock your machine, or close unsaved work.

Not a mouse-taking agent

Jarvis Mode uses safe OS window actions. No random clicking through websites, no autonomous task execution.

Not rigid forever rules

Your active pursuit changes the rules. YouTube can be research for Cooking and a distraction for Tech Job.

Local first

Activity is based on foreground apps and window titles. Screen pixels are not captured for the current Warden/Jarvis behavior.

Windows beta

The current download is an unsigned portable beta. Setup will tell you what it needs before installing local helpers.

Human controlled

Pesto helps you stay pointed at your own work. It does not do your job for you.
